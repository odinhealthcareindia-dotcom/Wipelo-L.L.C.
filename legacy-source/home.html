// Asked, answered

Questions people actually ask.

So it's… a wet wipe? +
Yes. We say it on the front of the pack. The difference is the word before it: functional. A baby wipe removes surface sweat and adds nothing. Each Wipelo SKU carries one treatment active with a stated function — pore clearance (BARE), odor control (FIRST) — formulated inside adult skin's pH window and sealed individually. If all you need is sweat removal, a $0.04 wipe genuinely does that. This is for the jobs.
How do I cancel or pause my subscription? +
In one click, from your account, no chat with a retention specialist. We email you 3 days before every shipment so you can swap SKUs, skip a month, or cancel before anything ships. Subscriptions should be a convenience, not a trap.
Which one should I start with? +
Most people start with FIRST — it's the multi-purpose one, and the one you'll reach for most. If body breakouts are the specific problem, start with BARE. The 10-second quiz above will match you, or take the guess out entirely with a bundle.
What does pH-True™ actually mean? +
Adult body skin sits at pH 4.5–5.5 — the acid mantle that keeps your barrier intact. Most soap sits near pH 10; most wipes never state theirs. Every Wipelo batch is formulated and tested inside the 4.5–5.5 window. That's the whole claim, and we can show it per batch.
Are the wipes flushable or biodegradable? +
The cloth is plant-fiber and biodegradable. It is not flushable — no wipe truly is, whatever the tub says. Bin it. Honesty is the brand; this is what it looks like on an unglamorous question.
What if it doesn't work for me? +
The Skin-Is-Skin Guarantee: 30 days, full refund, no questions, no return shipping. Fewer than 1% of customers ever use it* — we'd rather lose a sale than argue with your skin. (*dummy figure for prototype.)
