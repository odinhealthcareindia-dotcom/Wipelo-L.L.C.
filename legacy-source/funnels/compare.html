// The verdicts, honestly
Baby wipes — keep them if…

…you have a baby. Genuinely excellent at their actual job. On adult skin they remove sweat and add nothing: no active, no pH logic, no odor chemistry.

Best for: infants · Not for: your gym bag
"Fresh-scent" body wipes — keep them if…

…you want to smell like the wipe. Perfume layers over odor; by afternoon you have both. Rarely state their pH; almost never carry a dosed active.

Best for: masking · Not for: fixing
Bar soap — keep it if…

…you're standing in a shower. At pH ~10 it strips the acid mantle, but with water and a towel it's a fine reset. It just can't come to the meeting.

Best for: showers · Not for: 8:40 a.m.
Wipelo First — buy it if…

…you want odor removed at the source between showers, at your skin's real pH, from a sealed single that lives wherever you do. It costs more per use than everything above. That's the honest trade: engineering isn't four cents.

Function: odor control · zinc complex + aloe · pH-True™
