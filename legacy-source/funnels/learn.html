// Functional Wet Wipes™ — a definition

What makes a wet wipe functional?

"Functional" did something specific for beverages and mushrooms: it split a commodity into two categories — the one that's just there, and the one that does something. Wet wipes never got that split. Until now, every wipe was decorative: it moved moisture around and added perfume.

A functional wet wipe meets four tests:

01A stated functionOn the pack, in mono
02One active, dosedNamed + concentration
03pH inside 4.5–5.5Batch-tested
04Sealed per wipeThe 50th = the 1st

Test a baby wipe against those four and it scores zero — which is fine, because it was engineered for a three-week-old. Test a "fresh-scent" body wipe and it fails on all four too, which is less fine, because it was marketed to you.

"Below the jaw, you've been on your own — improvising with products built for infants, hospitals, or no one."

Wipelo makes four functional wipes: BARE (pore clearance — salicylic acid 2%), FIRST (odor control — zinc complex + aloe), HER (microbiome support — cranberry + lactic acid, reviewed with gynecological input), and CLEAN (pure removal — 100% cotton, no actives). One word names the territory; the spec sheet names the science.
